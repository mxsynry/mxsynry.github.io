Methodology

Two sources. One honest view.

Synchrose joins entries by a normalized product name. WEAO supplies live compatibility, detection, version, and Roblox build fields. Voxlis enriches those rows with platforms, catalog tags, summaries, and pricing context.

When a source is missing or disagrees, the interface keeps the uncertainty visible instead of inventing certainty.

01

Compatibility

Updated, detected, version, and current Roblox build signals come from the WEAO public API.

02

Catalog context

Descriptions, tags, platforms, and price offers come from the public Voxlis catalog repository.

03

Source labels

Every card says WEAO, Voxlis, or Both so you can judge the provenance at a glance.
